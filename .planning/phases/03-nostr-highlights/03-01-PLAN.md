## Phase Goal

**As a** logged-in reader, **I want to** highlight selected article text as a NIP-84 event and see my marks for that URL, **so that** reading stays first and Boris never holds my user key.

<objective>
Ship own NIP-84 highlights in the reader on the stored Session. Logged-in user selects title or body, taps Highlight next to Copy, signs kind 9802 through Amber or bunker, sees the quote marked, and sees existing own 9802s for that URL. Logged out, the reader is unchanged. Reading stays ungated.

</objective>
